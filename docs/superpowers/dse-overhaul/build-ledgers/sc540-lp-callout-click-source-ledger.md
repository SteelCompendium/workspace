## Ticket facts (description, filed 2026-11-02 by the SC-537 owner)
- "Found by the SC-537 diagnosis in real Obsidian 1.15.0, on dse develop 458abe3."
- Problem: "in Live Preview, any click on a DSE control inside a `> [!note]` callout (for example + on a `ds-counter`) also selects the whole callout. The callout stops drawing as a card and shows its markdown source until the cursor leaves it. The Enter key that opens the block edit form from the pencil does the same. A plain code block outside a callout does not."
- Cause (measured): "DSE's click shield (`src/framework/pipeline.ts`, `armClickShield`) stops `mousedown`/`pointerdown` but not `click`. Obsidian's callout widget listens for `click` and selects the callout unless the target is inside `.cm-editor, .interactive-child` or the click was `defaultPrevented`."
- Fix candidates: "add `interactive-child` to the DSE root, or stop `click` from bubbling out of it."
- Evidence: build-ledgers/sc537-lp-callout-twins/sc537-r1-diag-report.md §2.5 (callout widget `hookClickHandler` app.js 1.15.0 @1764204 via `Ah` @820778 -> `selectElement` @1765049 with `scrollIntoView: true`).
- Thread: 0 comments at start (2026-11-02).
