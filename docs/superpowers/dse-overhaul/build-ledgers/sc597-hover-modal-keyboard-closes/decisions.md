## Ticket text (verbatim, the spec)

> Hover over a link to a note that holds a stamina bar. Focus the bar in the preview and press Enter (or Space). The stamina modal opens, then closes by itself about a third of a second later, with no click and no other input. The hover preview closes with it.
>
> Opening the same modal with a mouse click does not do this. Found while diagnosing SC-595 (a mouse click inside a form opened from a hover preview closes it).

Agent notes (verbatim): Measured by the SC-595 r1 reviewer in real Obsidian 1.15.1, Reading view and Live Preview, dse develop `eaa8742a`: modal open at 406/418 ms, the popover's `hide()` at 710/722 ms with no event, from the hover timer `setTimeout(() => shouldShow() ? transition() : hide(), waitTime)` (app.js:509 col ~107969). Cause: only a click through the preview runs Obsidian's `p()` (`setIsFocused(true)`); a keyboard open never focuses the popover, so the hover timer sees the pointer outside `hoverEl` and hides it, and the modal closes with the popover's view (SC-552 keep-open rule; note not open in a pane). SC-595's fix (DSE modals stop their own clicks from reaching the popover's window click listener) does not cover this path; a fix likely needs the private `setIsFocused` or another way to make the popover count as focused.
Report: `docs/superpowers/dse-overhaul/build-ledgers/sc595-hover-form-click-closes/sc595-r1-diagnosis-report.md` (§3 row "No input at all", §1 table; logs `sc595-r1-probe-E-stamina-keyboard*.log`).
