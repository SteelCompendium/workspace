## Ticket text (verbatim, the spec)

> Hover over a link to a note that holds a stamina bar. Click the bar's numbers in the preview to open the Stamina window, and click inside the window again very quickly (a fast double-click is enough). The window and the preview both close.
>
> A click a quarter of a second or more after the window opens is fine (SC-595 fixed that case). Nothing typed can be lost, because the window has only just opened.

Agent notes (verbatim): Found by the SC-595 r5 re-reviewer in real Obsidian 1.15.1 (dse `a53b0454` and `74b138f0`, i.e. present before SC-595's final fix too). A press 83-169 ms after the opening click closes everything; 255 ms or later is fine; opening by a click on the bar itself (not its number text) is fine. Cause: Obsidian's window-capture `T3` click handler (app.js:509) hides a popover that is not yet "focused" (`isFocused` is set by Obsidian's click-to-edit helper, about 150 ms after a click on text). SC-595's guard (bubble-phase `stopPropagation` at the modal's document) cannot stop a window-capture listener. Likely fix shared with SC-597: mark the popover focused (`setIsFocused(true)`, private API) when a DSE modal opens from it.
Report: `docs/superpowers/dse-overhaul/build-ledgers/sc595-hover-form-click-closes/sc595-r5-rereview-report.md` (LOW-2), preserved at SC-595's landing.
