## 3. The rule and its properties (add to the SC-607 / SC-612 notes)
- **Rule P at the rebase for twinned part pins** (`rebaseCardPin`, `ReadingModeBlockHost.ts`): after `mapLineStrict` agrees with the link's line, a block twinned in the note it was drawn from OR in the note now (`twinned(whole, drawnPin)`) is placed only when `placedUniquely(whole, {text: from.text, lineStart: from.line}, at.line)` holds, i.e. SC-612's rule at the drawn -> now step. `mapLineStrict` and its tail-first check are unchanged. Reason: both maps trim an untouched side first, so a change on ONE side of a run of identical blocks leaves them agreeing on the survivor and the write-time rule P compared now with now.
- **Repeat-until-reopen property (accepted, ruled by the owner on SC-607 F1/F3 and SC-621 Q1):** a refusal here repeats on every click of a preview or embed that is not redrawn, until it is reopened (measured: click 1 refused + 1 Notice, click 2 refused + 1 Notice, a card mounted on the note now saves with no Notice). A twin-gate test pins it (`reading-mode-host-part-pin-twin.test.ts`, "persistence").
- **Residuals (accepted, base writes the same; no rule over the text can see R1 and R2, R3 is too large for the plugin to price):** R1 a move among text-identical blocks (the note's text does not change at all, SC-612 N5/N6 class); R2 the drawn-from section deleted while a neighbour is renamed to its name, where the cheapest reading keeps the drawn-from counter (same layering as SC-607 F4); **R3** the ticket's shape combined with a very large outside change: more than LINE_MAP_MAX_EDITS (400) lines changed around the block in the same write, so `placedUniquely` cannot price it and the agreed map stands (SC-612's accepted cap; r3 measured 474 such T1 cells, all with a drawn -> now distance of at least 401; a code variant "refuse when twinned and over the cap" stopped only 9 of 132 other-counter writes and added 92 unambiguous refusals, so it was not taken). The sweep pins the R1/R2 cell counts (R3 is not in the committed sweep subset).
- The twin gate must be `twinned(whole, drawnPin)` (the note as drawn OR now): a gate on the drawn note only lets an identical counter pasted above a UNIQUE drawn-from counter (+ TOP edited) through. The named twin test "an identical counter pasted right above the unique one the card was drawn from, TOP edited" kills that mutant (measured, `sc621-r2-gate-mutant-M2.log`: 2 failed).
