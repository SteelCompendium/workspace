## Ticket text (verbatim)

> With the cursor in a tracker text box (for example the Malice quick-add amount), a mouse press on a roster cell selects that creature and focus falls to the page body instead of moving to the cell.
>
> Found while fixing SC-609, which fixed the same press when focus is on a button. Already the case before SC-609. Likely solved together with SC-458 (a click on a card control does not move focus to it). Low (accessibility).
>
> Agent notes: SC-609 r1 survey probe S03. The element click shield (`armClickShield`, `src/framework/pipeline.ts:~253-259` at dse `231f2a3b`) deliberately blurs a focused editable control on pointerdown so click-away saves the draft. By the time the roster cell's `click` handler runs, focus is already on body, so SC-609's "was focus inside the tracker" check (`captureControlFocus` in the cell `onClick`, `view.ts`) sees nothing. Fix needs an earlier capture (at pointerdown, before the shield) or a shield change that moves focus to the press target (SC-458).

## Owner rulings

- 2026-11-13 — **No Scott decision needed.** The ticket states the expected behaviour ("instead of moving to the cell"), and it is the same rule SC-609 already applies when focus is on a button.
- 2026-11-13 — **Behaviour.** When keyboard focus is in a text box inside the tracker (the Malice quick-add amount or label, or any other editable text box inside the tracker that the click shield blurs) and a mouse press on a roster cell selects that creature, focus lands on the REBUILT pressed roster cell — exactly SC-609's outcome for a focused button. Focus does NOT go back into the text box. If focus was NOT inside the tracker before the press, nothing changes (SC-588/SC-609: "A mouse click never pulls focus into the tracker").
- 2026-11-13 — **Keep the click shield's job.** The shield's blur on pointerdown (so click-away commits/saves an editable draft) must keep happening exactly as today. What is typed in the Malice quick-add boxes must survive the press (SC-589 draft: amount and label text kept), and a half-typed editable stepper value must still be committed by that blur as today. The fix only adds "remember that focus was inside the tracker" early enough for the cell's click to see it.
- 2026-11-13 — **Approach.** Smallest change that makes SC-609's existing check see the pre-shield focus: e.g. a record taken at pointerdown before the shield blurs (a capture-phase listener in the tracker, or the shield noting which editable it blurred for that press so `captureControlFocus`'s caller can read it). Any framework touch must be a no-op for every element other than the initiative tracker's roster cell path, and must not move focus anywhere new. The general "a click on a card control moves focus to it" (SC-458) is OUT OF SCOPE — do not change the shield to focus press targets in general.
- 2026-11-13 — **Scope.** Reading view and the pinned sidebar tracker (required, both tested). Live Preview: report what happens (SC-602 carries focus across the element's own save; if the selection press writes, the carry may or may not land on the cell) — if it works, say so; if not, it is a follow-up, not this fix. Survey: list every editable text box in the tracker the shield blurs and say for each whether a roster press now lands on the cell (amount, label, anything else found).
