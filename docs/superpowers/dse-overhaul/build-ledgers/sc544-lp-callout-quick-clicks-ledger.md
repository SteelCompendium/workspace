## Ticket facts (description, verbatim; filed 2026-11-02 by SC-537's owner from SC-537 r3 MEDIUM-2)
- "Found by the SC-537 r3 review in real Obsidian 1.15.0. Also happens on dse develop f81a277, before SC-537, so the SC-537 fix doesn't cause it and doesn't fix it."
- "**Steps:** in Live Preview, three identical callouts, each holding the same `ds-counter` at 5. Click + on the upper one, then about 120 ms later click + on the middle one."
- "**Result:** disk goes [5,5,5] → [6,5,5] → **[6,5,6]**. The middle's click was written into the LOWER block. Until the next edit, the middle card shows 6 while its block holds 5. Three quick clicks give [6,5,7]. Two callouts, or slower clicks, are fine."
- "**Why:** the upper's write makes Obsidian redraw the callouts. The middle card moves to the lower slot while its +1 is still waiting to be saved (400 ms debounce), and the save then goes to the block it now sits on. SC-537's check compares a card's last-known text with the block under it, so it can't see this: the waiting card still holds the same text as its twins."
- "**Fix idea (from the review):** in the editor pass, remember where each Live Preview callout card's widget was, map that position through each edit (`update.changes.mapPos`), and treat a card whose widget now sits somewhere else as moved."
- Evidence: SC-537 ledger (preserved) `docs/superpowers/dse-overhaul/build-ledgers/sc537-lp-callout-twins/sc537-r3-review-report.md` MEDIUM-2 (lines 130-147), probes `sc537-r3-probes.log` (`fast3-01-120`, `fast3-012-120`, clean: `fast3-12-120` and all two-callout cases). Reviewer's fix detail (report :144-146): remember each LP callout host's widget position (`posAtDOM`) per editor; on the next update compare `update.changes.mapPos(prev)` with current `posAtDOM(widget)`; if different, release and redraw even if bodies are equal; "Flush that view's pending edit to the block it was clicked on (its mapped previous line), not by body: a body search is ambiguous between twins (SC-480)." Not closed by SC-537's docChanged/viewportChanged/selectionSet guard.
- Reviewer's mechanism note (report :137): "the upper's write rebuilds the callouts. The middle callout is raw from its own click, so it consumes the upper's old widget. The lower slot gets the middle's widget and R21 with its pending +1." The "raw from its own click" part is SC-540's symptom (a click on a DSE control in an LP callout selects the whole callout) — interaction to be established in r1.
