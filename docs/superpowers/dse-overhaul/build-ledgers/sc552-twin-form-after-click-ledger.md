## Ticket facts (description, verbatim; filed 2026-11-02 by the SC-544 owner from SC-544 r3 MEDIUM-1)
- "Found by the SC-544 r3 review in real Obsidian 1.15.0. Same on dse develop 423e798d (before SC-544) and on the SC-544 branch; SC-544 does not fix it."
- "**Steps:** three identical blocks holding the same `ds-counter` (callouts or plain fences, Live Preview or Reading view). Click + on the upper one, then within about 400 ms open the MIDDLE block's edit form (pencil), change the name, and Save."
- "**Result:** disk [6,5,5], names [Twin, Twin, **Edited**]. The edit lands in the LOWER block; the middle block is unchanged. Nothing is erased, but the edit is in the wrong block. Opening the form after the click's save has landed is fine."
- "**Why:** the upper's save makes Obsidian redraw the identical blocks and hand the middle's card to the lower slot while the form is open. The form's Save then goes to the block that card now sits on. SC-544 fixed the same mechanism for waiting clicks; an open form is a different kind of \"waiting\"."
- "**Fix idea (from the review):** when a form opens, first save the note's other waiting clicks and wait for that save to settle — SC-519's `openAfterPendingWrite`, widened from \"this card\" to \"this note\". The form then opens on a card that sits on its own block. Residual: a write that lands while the form is open (an outside edit, Sync) can still move it."
- Evidence: `.superpowers/sdd/sc544-lp-callout-quick-clicks/sc544-r3-review-report.md` MEDIUM-1 (line 38 on), probes `formA-lp`, `formA-rv`, `formA-pl-lp`, `formA-slow-lp` in `sc544-r3-probes.log`. Mechanism there: `ReadingModeBlockHost.ts:683-707`, the form's `replaceSource` (`fromView: false`) resolves its section through the host's element at Save (`readSection` :706); the view has nothing waiting so `flushPendingWrites` :701 does nothing.
- "**Related, script-only today (SC-544 r5 review):** with a form open on the middle block and the middle card's own click still waiting, the card's later save erases the form's edit (values [6,6,6], names all Twin). Same on base. A real user cannot reach it now because the form is a modal, but a fix here should keep it in mind."

## Owner caution on the review's fix idea (2026-11-02, for r1 to test, not a ruling)
Per SC-544's mechanism, after the upper's write settles, the clicked (middle) view's element may itself sit on the LOWER block. Flushing then opening "on this view" could still open the form on the lower block. r1 must measure where the clicked view sits after the settle before the idea is accepted.
